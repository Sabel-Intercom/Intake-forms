How this page works

Internal Ops is the one place that holds what we know about every client: who they are, what we are building, where they are up to, and the dates. Everything else (the Board, the trackers, the Kick-off Desk, the Task Hub) reads from here. Nikki runs it day to day; if she is away, this page tells you where things stand and what to do.

The tabs

  • Overview: every client in delivery as a card, grouped by where they are in the journey. "Needs attention" at the top is the chase list: overdue gates, open critical steps, blocked tracker tasks. Gate dates are worked out from the Signed, Kickoff, Go-live and Day-30 dates; to change one gate only, open it and use Move this date. To pause a whole client, use Put on hold.
  • Board: one row per client, grouped by how close their go-live is. Progress, overdue and blocked counts come from the client's tracker. Use it on the Monday call.
  • Today: Nikki's own steps that are due, across all clients.
  • Sales follow-up: clients we have pitched to and are waiting on. Not in delivery.
  • Checklists: the standard steps behind each gate. Editing here changes the template for every client.
  • The (i) on a step: what the step is, why it exists (which client taught us) and how to do it. Written for whoever is doing the step for the first time. Steps without an (i) are self-explanatory.

Project types

Build (an Intercom build, no migration), Migration (data in, no build), Build + Migration (both), Audit, and Consultation only (hours of advice, no delivery journey; its date on the Board is the next check-in, set in Details). The type picks which gate list the client follows.

Flownetic projects

Greg's own projects, run through Sabel. Set "Delivered by" to Flownetic in Details. Greg manages them, so there is no tracker and no data to fill in: the steps are the Slack thread, Rocketlane and a weekly update that opens again every Monday. If the type is Migration or Build + Migration, Richard is doing migration work and the migration gates apply too, without the signing and paperwork steps. Taking the mark off puts the client back as they were.

On hold

"Park" on the Board and "Put on hold" on the client are the same thing. A held client leaves the Board and the Today list, keeps everything recorded, and comes back with "Resume". The Task Hub pauses the client too: none of their tracker or Notion tasks show or count as overdue for the team until you resume them here. Always give a reason and a check-back date.

If you need to do something while Nikki is away

  • Pick your name in "Working as" at the top first, so the change is recorded under you.
  • A client signed: "+ Add client", choose "Signed, start at Day 0", the type and the lead. The runbook steps appear on the client.
  • A client is stuck: open them, "Put on hold", say why and when to check back.
  • A go-live moved: change it in the tracker, not here.
  • Something looks wrong: the warnings panel on each client says what is missing and where to fix it. Leave a note on the step rather than guessing.

Things that look like problems but are not

  • A client is missing from Overview: scoping, on hold and closed clients sit in folded groups at the bottom; sales follow-ups are under their own tab.
  • The band says Onboarding but the stage says Active: the band follows the gates, the stage can be set by hand. Tick the gates the client has passed, or use "Change starting gate".
  • "rebuilt" on a Board row: the tracker was rebuilt after its last save, so it shows its freshly built plan. Statuses ticked before the rebuild are gone from the tracker too.
  • "Not saved, will retry" in the top right: the connection dropped. Leave the tab open; it retries. Changes are kept in this browser meanwhile.

Import client inputs from the SOW
