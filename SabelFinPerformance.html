All columns cover the last 30 days, except "Since go-live" and "Change". Fin resolution rate = conversations Fin resolved ÷ conversations Fin took part in. Green 50%+, amber 30 to 49%, red under 30%. Change = last 30 days minus since go-live, in percentage points. Clients live under 30 days show "<30d": their 30-day columns start at go-live, so migrated or test conversations are left out. Fin involved = share of all conversations Fin took part in. Automation rate = conversations Fin resolved ÷ all conversations, so it shows how much of the total workload Fin takes off the team (resolution rate only counts the ones Fin joined). Handover rate = share of Fin conversations passed to a person. First reply = average hours to a first reply from a person. First-contact resolution = whole workspace, people and Fin. Under 20 Fin conversations, scores are shown faded: treat them as early signs. Go-live comes from each client's project tracker (hover the date to see the source). Hover any column heading for its full name. Clients without a tracker need a date set by hand. New Intercom workspaces are added automatically and marked New.
